Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

HP Slice Backup is a personal, self-hosted utility for saving home-server backup archives to the owner's Google Drive account. It is not offered as a public service.

Information accessed

When the owner authorizes Google Drive access, the utility may access the Google account authorization information and Google Drive files permitted by the scopes granted during authorization. It uses that access to upload and manage backup archives and, when needed, restore them.

Storage and sharing

Backup archives are stored in the owner's Google Drive account. Authentication tokens and local configuration are stored on the owner's self-hosted system. The utility does not sell personal information or intentionally share backup data with third parties. Google processes data according to its own terms and privacy policy.

Retention and control

The owner controls backup retention and can delete backup archives from Google Drive. Google account access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account security settings. Local authentication data can be removed from the self-hosted system.

Security

Access is limited to the owner's systems and Google account authorization. As with any self-hosted backup solution, the owner is responsible for protecting credentials and backup archives.

Contact

For questions, contact the project owner through the GitHub repository.
